In InterSystems Supply Chain Orchestrator, a business process is a long-running, graphical workflow (authored in Business Process Language, BPL) that orchestrates the steps of a supply chain operation — receiving a request, transforming data, calling other hosts, making decisions, and returning a response, all with full message tracing.
