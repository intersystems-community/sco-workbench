A business process automates a supply chain workflow in Supply Chain Orchestrator. Business processes can be used in many ways—from data integration logic in a pipeline and standard operating procedures (SOPs) for supply chain disruptions to automated agentic workflows. A process can be triggered by almost anything: a file arriving, a call from another system, a scheduled event, or an issue identified by SCO. It then applies your business rules and delivers the results to the people and systems that need them, supporting use cases such as interoperability workflows, issue analysis, decision intelligence, and automated actions.

Issue analysis: turning SOPs into decisions

Every supply chain team has SOPs for handling problems: how serious is this late order, who needs to know, and what are our options? An issue analysis business process applies those SOPs to every issue SCO finds. Each issue arrives rated, explained, and with resolution options, so your team spends its time deciding instead of investigating.
