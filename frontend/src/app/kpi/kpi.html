Business KPI

Overview

SCO enables users to define business Key Performance Indicator (KPI) to measure supply chain performance and identify issues. KPIs can include formulas, dimensional breakdowns such as supplier or region, and configurable thresholds or conditions for issue detection. KPIs run directly on SCO data, providing near-real-time results for monitoring, analysis, and decision-making.

Beyond SCO Workbench

SCO Workbench covers common KPI definitions. Some advanced capabilities require working directly in SCO:

Custom KPI calculation logic beyond standard cube measures or dimensions
Triggering or updating KPI generated issues
Upcoming new KPI features, such as KPI history, trend support
How to Use
1
Explore out-of-the-box KPIs
SCO ships with pre-built KPIs for common supply chain metrics. Select any KPI from the list on the left to inspect its definition, thresholds, and the issues it generates. Out-of-the-box KPIs are read-only.
2
Create a new KPI
Click + in the left panel. Define the KPI name, select the underlying cube and measure, set dimension breakdowns, and configure thresholds. Use the AI Assistant for help selecting the right measure or structuring your thresholds.
3
Edit a saved KPI
Select an existing KPI from the list to update its definition or thresholds. Changes take effect on the next KPI evaluation run.
Before creating a KPI, make sure the relevant Analytics Cube exists and has the data loaded. Ask the AI Assistant if you need help choosing the right cube or measure for your metric.
