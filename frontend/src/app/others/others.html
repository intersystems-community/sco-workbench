The following SCO features are not covered in the current version of SCO Workbench. They are available directly through SCO APIs, the Management Portal, or dedicated SCO interfaces. This page provides an overview to help you understand what is available and where to learn more.

📊
Scenario Analysis
Available in SCO · Not in Workbench

Scenario Analysis allows supply chain teams to model and compare alternative outcomes before committing to a decision. Users can define hypothetical changes — such as a supplier disruption, demand spike, or lead time increase — and evaluate their impact across KPIs and operational metrics. SCO provides a built-in framework for designing scenario templates and running side-by-side comparisons.

🤖
ML-Based Forecasting
Available in SCO · Not in Workbench

SCO integrates with InterSystems IntegratedML to bring machine learning forecasting directly into the supply chain platform. Teams can train predictive models on historical SCO data — such as demand patterns, lead times, or inventory levels — and use model outputs to drive proactive supply chain decisions without requiring external data science tooling.

✦
AI Assistant for Business Users
Available in SCO · Not in Workbench

Beyond the developer-focused AI Assistant in SCO Workbench, SCO provides a business-user-facing AI assistant embedded in the operational dashboard. It enables supply chain analysts and planners to query data, surface insights, and receive decision support using natural language — without writing queries or navigating complex interfaces.

📍
Track & Trace Service
SCO Cloud Service · Not in Workbench

The Track & Trace service is a cloud-hosted SCO capability that provides end-to-end visibility into the movement of goods across your supply chain network. It aggregates shipment events from multiple carriers and logistics partners into a unified timeline, enabling real-time tracking, exception detection, and estimated arrival prediction — accessible via API or the SCO dashboard.
