Issue Management

Overview

SCO supports issue detection through KPIs and business processes, as well as importing issues from external systems. It manages the issue lifecycle by analyzing severity, urgency, and impact, and provides actionable resolution insights through client-specific SOPs, scenario analysis, optimization, and AI/ML. SCO can also automate resolution actions or keep users in the loop when human intervention is needed.

Beyond SCO Workbench

SCO Workbench currently covers viewing issues identified by KPIs. The following issue management capabilities are not covered:

Build and run business processes to systematically analyze issues
Generate and track additional issues based on business events or the impact of mitigation actions
Run scenario analysis to evaluate potential actions and outcomes for an issue
Execute actions associated with decisions, either automatically or with a human in the loop
How to Use
1
Browse by category
Click a category in the left panel (By KPI, By Severity, or Work Queue) to filter the issue list. The number beside each category is how many issues it holds.
2
Review issues in the table
Each row shows the issue description, the record it affects, the triggering KPI, severity, urgency and status. Severity and urgency read "Not set" until SCO assigns them.
3
Open an issue for details
Click any row to open the issue, including the impacted record, the issue data recorded by the analysis process, and the resolution note once it is closed.
